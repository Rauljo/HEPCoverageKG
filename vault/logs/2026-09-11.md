## Decided

- D-178: the completeness check is asked of the model, once a round, and is
  never shown the draft answer -- only the retrieved rows. Pan et al. and
  Huang et al. both show intrinsic self-correction degrades correct answers;
  the rows are the external signal that keeps this out of that regime.
- D-179: a job whose name contains `reflect` exits 6 if the mode is off, and
  the header prints `reflect=`, `subgoals=`, `status_call=`.
- The check gets its own endpoint (`REFLECT_BASE_URL` / `REFLECT_MODEL`),
  defaulting to the critic's. It is extraction, not reasoning, and running it
  on QwQ once a round timed out 83% of records.
- A verdict that says READY:no while every part has something behind it is
  rendered as "you may answer", not as a gap. qwen3-32b said READY:no on all
  eleven verdicts and named an empty part on four.
- Every record now carries `subgoal_scope` and `reflect_mode`, because three
  paper36 runs with the same flags and config hash could not be told apart.
