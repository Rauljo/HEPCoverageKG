**What Swanson gives the payoff arm — four transfers** (full detail in `literature.md` [1]):
1. **The canonical citation for our hardest caveat.** Gap-hypothesis constraint 2 and the
   asymmetric-reliability caveat in held-out validation are special cases of his *essential
   incompleteness of information retrieval*. Turns a weakness into a stated position.
2. **A Popperian frame for act three.** Our enumeration IS a "search function" = a conjecture about
   World 3: falsifiable, never verifiable → the posture is **refutation-based** ("survived a severe
   test", never "verified absent"). Held-out validation becomes a severity-of-test argument; his
   Example 3 (weak tests jointly strong) is the vocabulary for aggregating weak ranking signals.
3. **The positioning sentence.** *Swanson's gap is epistemic (the knowledge exists, unassembled);
   ours is ontic (the measurement was never made).* But Stage-B's literature check IS Swanson-shaped,
   so the project holds both notions at different stages. Also usable: his **disjointness test** (no
   common authors, no mutual citations) computed on the HEP author/citation graph.
4. **Unexpected**: his universal-thesaurus infinite regress argues *against* pre-authoring an alias
   vocabulary — i.e. **for** the bottom-up, corpus-driven, human-gated `aliases/` layer built today.

**Two new ideas filed (Raul's, from GAPMAP)**
- **Mine "Future Work"/"Outlook" sections for author-declared gaps** → [[gap-hypothesis-system]] new
  section. A **prior/ranking signal** over structurally-enumerated cells, never the enumerator
  (constraint 1 holds). Declared gaps *outside* the grid = evidence of a **missing coverage axis**
  (log-only, leftovers-style). By-product: declared-at-*t* → filled-at-*t′* is a **timestamped
  labelled gap set** — our version of ResearchLink's CSKG-600, for free.
- **The gap-matching problem** → [[held-out-gap-validation]]. "Is this fill the same gap?" is where
  held-out validation lives or dies; exact matching under-counts fills and the bias *flatters* the
  finder. Reuse **aliases Tiers 2–3** (BGE blocking → cross-encoder *vs* LLM adjudication → human
  gate); precision-first, since a false fill *erases* a real gap. Plus an AGATHA-style **temporal
  holdout** using the future-work labels. Gives the backlog dedup-literature search a second consumer.
