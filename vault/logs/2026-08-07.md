## His tiers are by CAPABILITY; ours are by what makes truth safe

| his | what it measures |
|---|---|
| 1 · facet-index lookups | set operations on the analysis cards, **zero LLM calls expected** |
| 2 · graph traversal | multi-hop, `signature_derived`, the review lifecycle |
| 3 · evidence-text mining | answer verbatim in stored quotes — cite or fail |
| 4 · back to the paper | deliberately NOT in the graph: recognise the boundary, then ground in HTML |

Complementary to Tier A / Tier B / retrieval, not competing.
**Q5 is a deliberate trap** — "which analyses reconstruct a Higgs-boson *candidate* as a detector
object?" — where string-matching "Higgs" drowns in Higgs *process* papers. That is exactly the
weakness measured on 2026-08-04 (retrieval 0.893 named vs **0.335** described), so **we should expect
to fail it**, and it will be a clean demonstration of why the relevance step matters.
**Tier 4 is our abstention design, better specified**: two-part pass criterion, and we have the
2,969-paper corpus on DIAS for the grounding half.

## The measurement that reordered the plan

**9 of his 15 questions need data we have not imported.** Tier 1 is set operations over

backlog since 2026-07-27, and it is small — 60 rows, 49 KB.

## One ladder, not three systems (S-68 … S-70)

The day's idea, and the user's: facets, aliases and grouping are **rungs of one abstraction ladder**.

```
raw 227  ->  canonical 194  ->  families (not built)  ->  facet key 18     (detector objects)
```

- **Both measured failures are wrong-rung errors.** Tier B 0.058 = too coarse. His Q5 = too fine.
- **Facets are not better deduplication.** Dedup *discovers* identity and preserves what it does not
  merge; the facet map *classifies* and deliberately discards — `b-tagged jet (tight WP)` and
  `(relaxed WP)` were correctly rejected as a merge and both become `BJet`.
- **Coverage: 46%.** 2,375 of 5,114 entities have a facet field. `event_region` (771), `sample` (551),
  `result` (272), `channel` (208) have none — exactly where free vocabulary is worst.
- **Retrieve every rung, let the critic pick** (S-69). Routing before retrieval must guess the
  abstraction level before knowing what is there.
- **Validation is symmetric and already built**: his Tier 1 catches under-use of facets, our Tier B
  catches over-use. Neither set alone finds both.
- **Keeping the raw rung changes the risk of merging** (S-70). A wrong merge corrupts one rung, not
  the graph — "could silently ruin every count" becomes "could give a wrong answer to some
  questions". That loosens the caution holding the aliases layer at 342 merges, and makes grouping
  one more rung rather than a second thing to get right.

## A free by-product

For the 46% it covers, the facet mapping is **partial supervision for deduplication**: any two
entities landing on the same facet key are pairs the aliases layer arguably should merge. 2,375
entities' worth, unlabelled by anyone.
