at least one checkable claim, and \emph{checked} is printed beside the score.

harness never registers the values it returns, so no claim can be traced ---
mechanical provenance is a property of the typed interface, not of the graph
alone. Chained arms are excluded because the chain runner unions entities,
papers and evidence across legs but carries only the final leg's returned values,
so its trace covers a fraction of its own answer. Prose is deliberately not
checked: paraphrase is legitimate, and flagging it would bury the real findings.
The check therefore catches an invented paper or an invented number, not every
ungrounded sentence.}
